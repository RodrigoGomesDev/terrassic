## Regras (#rules)

**Welcome to Terrassic!** A dinosaur mod for Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric + Forge. Keep it friendly:

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
2. **Keep it safe for everyone.** Many players are young: no NSFW, gore or shock content.
3. **No piracy, no re-uploads.** Download Terrassic only from Modrinth or CurseForge.
4. **No spam or self-promotion** outside the channels made for it. No unsolicited DMs.
5. **Don't ask for release dates.** Updates ship when they are ready, and #announcements will say so.
6. **Bugs go to #bug-reports** with your Minecraft version, loader (Fabric/Forge), Terrassic version and the log.
7. **Original work only** in #fan-art. Credit other artists. No Jurassic Park/World material.
8. **Moderators have the final word.** Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

**Bem-vindo ao Terrassic!** Mod de dinossauros para Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric + Forge. As regras acima valem
para todos; quem fala português tem a categoria 🇧🇷 PORTUGUÊS.
