# Summary (até 256 caracteres)

Life-size dinosaurs you can tame, ride and fly. 59 original creatures, a Cretaceous world, a scientist's Field Notebook and a story through time. Fabric + Forge, 1.20.1.

# Descrição

![Terrassic](BANNER_URL)

**Terrassic** is a dinosaur mod for **Minecraft 1.20.1** on **Fabric and Forge**, made from scratch: every model, animation, texture, sound and line of story is original.

> 🦖 **Early alpha.** Things will change and break between versions. Back up your worlds and report bugs on Discord.

## The Leap

The Terrassic Project built a machine to observe the past. Dr. Katarina Usha stepped through and never came back. You go in after her, the machine fails, and you wake up in the Late Cretaceous with nothing. Follow her notes, survive with stone, bone and flint, and rebuild the Leap Altar. The story guides you but never moves you on its own: eras stay open, and if you only want dinosaurs, you can ignore it.

## What's in

- **59 creatures at real scale.** 1 block = 1 metre, sized from the largest known fossils: Tyrannosaurus, Spinosaurus, Giganotosaurus, Triceratops, Argentinosaurus, Quetzalcoatlus, Mosasaurus and many more.
- **Males and females**, breeding, **eggs that hatch** and babies that imprint on the nearest player.
- **Taming and 32 mounts**: walk with a titanosaur, charge on a ceratopsian, fly a Quetzalcoatlus, swim with a Mosasaurus.
- **A living ecosystem**: herds, flocks, hunting predators, nests, fish in rivers and seas, and Cretaceous game animals in place of the vanilla ones.
- **The Terrassic world type**: 11 Cretaceous biomes, the Arrival Crater, Katarina's camps, fossil sites, the River House and Rift Storms.
- **Be a scientist**: dig fossils, sketch creatures and fill the **Field Notebook**. Each species you study is easier to tame and to fight.
- **Primitive building**: 500+ blocks from the age itself (limestone, flint, araucaria, cycads, thatch, wattle and daub, hide rugs, skull trophies), with no copies of vanilla blocks.
- **The first boss**: the Rift Tyrant, called by a meteor ritual at the Leap Altar. It scales with the number of players.
- **World rules** in-game, with sliders for spawns, taming, damage and more.
- In **English, Portuguese and Spanish**.
